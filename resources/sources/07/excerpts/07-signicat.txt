Follow the four main phases of preparing for EUDI Wallet integration:

1. Assessment: Assess whether eIDAS 2 or AMLR requirements apply, and map your identity use cases, channels, and required attributes.
2. Technical integration: Choose an integration strategy and protocol, connect to the wallet API, process incoming data, and design user journeys.
3. Relying Party registration: Register your organisation and intended use, obtain the required certificates, and establish data minimisation policies.
4. Testing and launch: Test authentication flows and different use cases and prepare to move your integration into production.

Signicat can support your organisation throughout the EUDI Wallet integration process, from expert-led guidance during your assessment process to a full suite of wallet-based solutions you can integrate into your application.

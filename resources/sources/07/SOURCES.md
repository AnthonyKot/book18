## Evidence boundary

No source reviewed establishes that a mid-size Dutch pre-employment screener will pay the proposed prices or keep paying after its incumbent identity provider adds EUDI acceptance. No source reviewed establishes a live Dutch production relying-party register, registration policy or certificate-issuance route on the access date. Those uncertainties remain explicit in the essay and the paid ninety-day test (`07-demand-gap`).

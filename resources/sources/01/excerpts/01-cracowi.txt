CRACoWi is an EU-funded project under the Digital Europe Program (DEP), launched in September 2024 to tackle the growing cybersecurity challenges in the European digital market. Bringing together 14 partners from Greece, Germany, Slovenia, and Cyprus, the project develops AI-powered tools to help SMEs, manufacturers, distributors, and importers comply with the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA).

Project starting date: September 1, 2024
Project end date: August 31, 2027
Project duration: 36 months
Project Value: €8,443,840.80
The project funded under Grant Agreement No.101158539 is supported by the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre.

CRACoWi, or the “Cyber Resilience Act - Compliance – Wizard" is being developed by the CRACoWi consortium to support SME manufacturers, distributors, and importers in meeting these conditions. Wizard emphasizes cybersecurity from a product's inception through its lifecycle, offering automated CRA compliance assessments, documentation generation, and enhanced product security.

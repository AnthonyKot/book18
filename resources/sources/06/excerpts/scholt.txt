Scholt works with renewable generation, battery storage and flexible industrial consumption, allowing flexibility to be assessed in its wider energy context.

Scholt combines asset information, agreed operating limits, market access and optimisation to determine when flexibility should be available or activated.

Scholt Energy connects these assets to the relevant energy markets through its Virtual Power Plant (VPP).

## Evidence boundary

The code record establishes the consequences a grid operator may impose after repeated GTO breaches. ACM declined only to prescribe civil agreements between connected parties or groups and grid operators. Allocation among a hub's members, installer and software provider remains a private-contract design question and is not attributed to ACM. No primary source found establishes that a blocked business park will pay the proposed diagnostic or recurring price; that an installer will preserve a separately priced wholesale line; or that a group will grant the required operating and evidence rights. Those questions remain the paid-test gates. Programme budgets and contract availability are not evidence of customer payment.

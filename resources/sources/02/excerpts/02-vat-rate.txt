There are 3 VAT tariffs in the Netherlands:

* 0% tariff
* 9% tariff
* 21% tariff

21% tariff

The 21% tariff is also called the high or general tariff. Is there no reason for an exemption, the reverse-charge mechanism, the 0% tariff or the 9% tariff? Then you must charge 21% VAT.

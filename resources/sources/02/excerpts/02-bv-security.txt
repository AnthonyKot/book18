Testing, Compliance and Certification of Industrial Control Systems (ICS)

Bureau Veritas Cybersecurity can provide testing and certification services in line with the relevant standards and regulations addressing the domain of ICS SCADA.

IEC 62443 assessments and certification

IEC 62443-4-2 and IEC 62443-3-3 can be used to validate the security of ICS products or integrated systems. IEC 62443-4-1 can be used to validate the security of the development processes.

We can offer you support in complying with the standard, as well as formal audits and certification based on IEC 62443.

The client contractually requires the credentials

If we deliver white-label work, does my company become ENS or ISO 27001 certified?

No. The certifications — ENS at HIGH category and the five ISO standards — belong to Hard2bit: they vouch for the quality of the engine operating behind your offering, but they are not inherited or transferred. Delivering white-label work does not make the partner a certified company. If your client contractually requires those credentials, the correct route is direct contracting with Hard2bit, with a commission for you.

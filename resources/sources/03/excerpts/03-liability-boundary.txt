Page/section: “Does the money work?” (proposed contractual and insurance design underlying inference)

The subcontract makes the vision company answer only when its module fails the signed protocol. It indemnifies the integrator for vision-company negligence, intellectual-property infringement and breach. The integrator reciprocates for mechanics, PLC, guarding and unauthorised changes. The customer retains recipe accuracy, bypass, release, holds, withdrawals and recalls. Consequential loss and retailer-claim indemnity are excluded unless priced.

Liability is assumed capped per cell at the €35,000 optical/software fees paid and annually at fees paid in the previous 12 months. Before installation, obtain a quote for at least €2 million combined professional-indemnity and product-liability cover. It must include software error, contractual liability and defence costs for food-production work. Record the deductible and any withdrawal or recall exclusion. These are design hurdles, not observed terms.

Page/section: “Field of View and Reading Distances”

The following maps show the field of view of the DataMan 360 series readers. Reading distance values are also provided for 1-D and 2-D example code distances.

For the C-mount (or other non-Cognex) and S-Mount lenses, the focal length of the lens, focus setting, and aperture setting determine the field of view and reading distance.

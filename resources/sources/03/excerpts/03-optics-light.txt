Page/section: “Adjust Image”, lighting types

Polarized lighting can help to minimize surface irregularities and reduce ambient light interference. It works best on direct part marking (DPM) codes with different surface finishes.

Diffuse lighting provides even illumination, which results in no glare. It is great for curved, shiny surfaces.

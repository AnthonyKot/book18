DPP timeline*

*This is an indicative timeline and subject to publication requirements.
Following the adoption of ESPR delegated acts, economic operators will
have a transition period of at least 18 months.

September 2026

Implementing Decision on the remaining two DPP standards

Q4 2026

Sector-specific DPP requirements for iron and steel and Implementing
Act for battery access rights

Adoption of the ESPR Delegated Act for Iron and Steel; Implementing
Act for Batteries access rights

Q2 2027

DPP requirements for construction products and service providers

Delegated Act for DPP Service Providers, and Delegated Act for
Construction Materials under the Construction Products Regulation
(CPR).

Q3 2027

DPP requirements for service providers

Delegated Act for DPP Service Providers

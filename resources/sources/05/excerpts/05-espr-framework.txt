Legal basis

The legal basis for the DPP is the Ecodesign Regulation (ESPR) (EU)
2024/1781. The specific DPP requirements for a product are defined in
one of two ways: either through the foreseen product-specific 'delegated
acts' adopted under the Ecodesign Regulation including

- 2026 – Iron and steel
- 2026–2029 – Energy-related products
- 2027 – Textiles, tyres, and aluminium
- 2028 – Furniture
- 2029 – Mattresses and ICT products

Or through separate, standalone EU legislation (such as Batteries
Regulation, Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), European
Critical Raw Materials Act, Toys Safety Regulation, Construction
Products Regulation and Detergents & Surfactants Regulation).

DPP timeline*

*This is an indicative timeline and subject to publication requirements.

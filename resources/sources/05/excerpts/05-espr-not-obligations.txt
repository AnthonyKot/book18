Page/section: “Plan to boost circular and efficient products on the European market”

It lists priority products for which the Commission will consider
setting ecodesign and energy labelling over the next five years. The
priority products, chosen for their potential to promote a transition to
a circular economy, are steel and aluminium, textiles (with a focus on
apparel), furniture, tyres and mattresses as well as a number of
energy-related products.

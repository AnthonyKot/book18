Page/section: “Guidance to support preparations for the Digital Batteries Passport”, “How to use the guidance”

The Guidance Document: Digital Batteries Passport – data points by
category provides a structured overview of the data points relevant to
electric vehicle (EV) batteries, light means of transport (LMT)
batteries and industrial batteries covered by the passport requirement.
For each battery category, it indicates whether individual data points
are mandatory, optional, applicable only in specific circumstances, or
do not have to be completed or displayed as of February 2027, when the
Batteries Passport requirements will begin to apply. In total, the
document brings together 71 data points, showing their legal sources and
their applicability to each battery category.

The guidance should be read together with Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and
any relevant delegated or implementing acts. It does not introduce
additional legal requirements and should not be regarded as an
authoritative interpretation of the legislation or as representing the
European Commission’s official position.

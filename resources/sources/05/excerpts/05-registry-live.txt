The DPP Registry

The DPP Registry is the EU database supporting the Digital Product
Passport system. It will be established at EU level and will store the
unique identifiers for each product, alongside mandatory registration
data as specified in the applicable legal act.

20 July 2026

DPP Registry becomes operational

The Digital Product Passport Registry becomes operational.

Step 2 - The DPP is created and registered

The DPP is created and registered in the Registry, while the complete
product information is stored by the economic operator or a DPP service
provider, together with the required DPP registration metadata. A
digital data carrier, such as a QR code, links the physical product to
its DPP.

20 July 2026

The Digital Product Passport Registry is now live

The European Commission launches the Digital Product Passport Registry
together with a testing environment, marking an important milestone in
making the Digital Product Passport (DPP) a practical reality for
businesses placing products on the EU market.

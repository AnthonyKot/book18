Page/section: Consolidated Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, Article 77(5), PDF page 84

5. All information included in the battery passport shall be based on
open standards and be in an interoperable format, transferable through
an open interoperable data exchange network without vendor lock-in,
machine-readable, structured and searchable, in accordance with the
essential requirements laid down in Article 78.

Circularise helps teams collect supplier evidence, trace battery data,
and publish governed QR-code disclosures without exposing sensitive
supply chain information.

Circularise brings those layers together across supplier data
collection, chain-of-custody traceability, and governed disclosure for
product passports.

Request, normalize, and review the declarations, certificates, and
field-level evidence needed for passport data.

Map battery passport fields to owners, suppliers, and systems, then
automate the evidence requests that usually slow teams down.

Link battery identifiers, batch history, supplier evidence, and
sustainability claims into an auditable data trail.

Expose the right passport data through QR codes, APIs, and partner
access while retaining control over sensitive fields.

Create a durable product record that can be linked to a physical battery
and updated through its lifecycle.

Keep the passport useful after sale with governed access to performance,
safety, and end-of-life information.

- Repair, reuse, and state-of-health updates

Plan integrations with ERP, PLM, LCA, and compliance systems so the
passport becomes a maintained record.

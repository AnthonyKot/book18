# Essay 5 — source index

Accessed and re-fetched 2026-09-02. Law and implementation status use EU primary sources. Vendor pages establish competitive product surface only; vendor “compliance” claims are not treated as legal conclusions.

1. **EUR-Lex, consolidated Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 concerning batteries and waste batteries, current consolidated text dated 31 July 2025.**
   URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A02023R1542-20250731
   Supports: passport scope/date and individual data; QR and marking; BMS health-data access; second-life status and responsibility; open/interoperable format; technical/access controls; due-diligence date, threshold and second-life exclusion (`05-art77-scope`, `05-qr-rule`, `05-second-life-transfer`, `05-operator-responsibility`, `05-art78-controls`, `05-bms-access`, `05-due-diligence-separate`). The consolidated text incorporates Regulation (EU) 2025/1561's due-diligence delay.

2. **European Commission, “Batteries” — Digital Product Passport.**
   URL: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/batteries_en
   Supports: Commission implementation summary, affected operator framing, decentralised design and 18 February 2027 date. Used as implementation context; the Regulation is the legal source.

3. **European Commission, “Guidance to support preparations for the Digital Batteries Passport,” 21 August 2026.**
   URL: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/news/guidance-support-preparations-digital-batteries-passport-2026-08-21_en
   Supports: current 71-data-point guidance and its explicit non-authoritative status (`05-71-points`).

4. **European Commission, “The DPP Registry.”**
   URL: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/dpp-registry_en
   Supports: current registry access and testing environment, registry contents, enforcement role and decentralised storage (supplementary support for `05-registry-live`).

5. **European Commission, “Digital Product Passport.”**
   URL: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport_en
   Supports: operational date and launch of the registry with a testing environment, decentralised storage, framework/delegated-act distinction, minimum transition statement, and implementation status on standards, battery access rights and service-provider rules (`05-registry-live`, `05-pending-rules`, `05-espr-framework`).

6. **European Commission, “Plan to boost circular and efficient products on the European market,” 11 July 2025.**
   URL: https://green-forum.ec.europa.eu/news/2025-2030-working-plan-2025-07-11_en
   Supports: ESPR 2025–2030 priority product groups (`05-espr-priorities`).

7. **Siemens, “Battery Passport API,” developer documentation.**
   URL: https://developer.siemens.com/battery-passport/overview.html
   Supports: REST API and user/passport management operations (`05-siemens-api`).

8. **Circularise, “Battery Passport.”**
   URL: https://www.circularise.com/battery-passport
   Supports: marketed supplier-evidence collection and review, field ownership and automated requests, traceability, governed access, lifecycle updates and source-system integrations (`05-circularise`).

9. **Minespider, product platform.**
   URL: https://www.minespider.com/
   Supports: marketed digital battery-passport tooling and “advanced API connectivity”; it does not support the earlier “open API” wording (`05-minespider`).

10. **Spherity, “Digital Product Passport Solution / VERA.”**
    URL: https://www.spherity.com/digital-product-passport
    Supports: marketed templates, APIs, access control and a white-label offer explicitly framed for consultancies and their clients (`05-spherity-product`, `05-spherity-channel`).

11. **TÜV SÜD, “EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542.”**
    URL: https://www.tuvsud.com/en-gb/services/product-certification/ce-marking/eu-battery-regulation
    Supports: candidate domain partner's marketed product-specific interpretation, battery testing and documented technical-verification services (`05-tuvsud-partner`). No partnership, availability or prospect introduction has yet been secured.

12. **EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 establishing the ESPR framework.**
    URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj
    Supports: primary legal context for the ESPR framework cited in the reading list; the Commission DPP page supplies the concrete delegated-act and transition claim (`05-espr-framework`).

## Evidence boundary

No source found establishes a market price or recurring external budget among small Benelux second-life battery operators. The prices, automation rate, labour minutes, workload capacity and financing bridge are explicit design hurdles. The Commission's future timetable is not treated as enacted where the referenced act had not been adopted on 2026-09-02. The ERP displacement row (`05-erp-substitute`) is an inference to test, not an externally verified product claim.

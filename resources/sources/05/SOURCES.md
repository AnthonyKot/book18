# Essay 5 — source index

Accessed 2026-09-01. Law and implementation status use EU primary sources. Vendor pages establish competitive product surface only; vendor “compliance” claims are not treated as legal conclusions.

1. **EUR-Lex, consolidated Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 concerning batteries and waste batteries, current consolidated text dated 31 July 2025.**
   URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1542/2025-07-31/eng/
   Supports: passport scope/date and individual data; QR and marking; BMS health-data access; second-life status and responsibility; open/interoperable format; technical/access controls; due-diligence date, threshold and second-life exclusion (`05-art77-scope`, `05-qr-rule`, `05-second-life-transfer`, `05-operator-responsibility`, `05-art78-controls`, `05-bms-access`, `05-due-diligence-separate`). ## Evidence boundary

No source found establishes a market price or recurring external budget among small Benelux second-life battery operators. The €12,000 sprint, €30,000 onboarding, €3,500 monthly retainer and conversion assumptions are explicit required-price tests. The Commission's stated future implementation timetable is not treated as an enacted deadline where the referenced act had not yet been adopted on 2026-09-01.

Industrial-grade IoT

Included in every subscription

Machine Health Critical combines the industry's most accurate AI with continuous sensing and expert validation to tell your team exactly what's failing, when to act, and what to do.

Machine Health solutions do exactly that, using IoT sensors that capture vibration, temperature, and magnetic data to provide real-time visibility into the condition, performance, and reliability of your industrial equipment.

The platform integrates with leading CMMS and EAM systems, including SAP PM, IBM Maximo, and Infor EAM, automatically triggering work orders complete with diagnostics and repair guidance so Machine Health insights flow directly into your existing maintenance workflows.

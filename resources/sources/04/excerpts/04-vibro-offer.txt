VIBRO IS A SPECIALIZED COMPANY OFFERING SERVICES IN THE FIELD OF:

- Condition monitoring (incl. telemonitoring) and maintenance advice for rotating machines.
- Vibration measurements including analyzes and independent advice.
- Calibration of all types of sensors and brands of vibration transducers.
- Calibration, service and maintenance of Schenck en Brüel and Kjaer Vibro (B&K Vibro GmbH) vibration measuring equipment and all brands of balancing machines.

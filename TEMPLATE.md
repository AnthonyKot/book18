# Book 18 — essay template

Kicker: `Essay N of 5 · Candidate name`

H1: an argued title, not merely the candidate label.

Lede (`p.lede`, no more than 130 words): the purchase decision and the tension that could
make the idea attractive or fatal.

1. **The proposition** (`section.proposition`) — one buyer, purchase event, deliverable,
   substitute and initial boundary. State what the business refuses to be.
2. **What changed** (`section.changed`) — dated regulation/technology/supply-chain change and
   evidence of practical buyer pain. Separate obligation, enforcement and budget.
3. **The first sale** (`section.sale`) — how ten named-prospect types would be reached, what
   fixed paid diagnostic is sold, who signs and why now. Address language and geography.
4. **Does the money work?** (`section.economics`) — bottom-up year 1 and plausible mature-year
   model. Separate sourced prices from required-price arithmetic; include working capital.
5. **From service to company** (`section.scale`) — first, fifth and twentieth customer; people,
   process, recurring work, accumulating asset, and founder bottleneck.
6. **The incumbent gets a turn** (`section.competition`) — named competitors and substitutes;
   how they respond; why partners might capture the customer or margin.
7. **The capable failure** (`div.counter`) — a non-straw-man path where good execution loses.
8. **The first ninety days** (`section.monday`) — 4–6 numbered actions, maximum pre-evidence
   spend, paid test, pass rule and kill rule.
9. **Verdict** (`div.verdict-box`) — one exact `CONTEXT.md` verdict and the single assumption
   that would most change it.
10. **Reading** (`div.reading`) — 5–10 primary/authoritative sources actually used.
